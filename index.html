Discover Basar's Hidden Gem

Located amidst the picturesque landscapes of Arunachal Pradesh, The Porup Campsite (founded under the proprietorship of Lige Nyodu) is your ideal getaway from the hustle of city life. Enjoy quiet evenings around the campfire, explore pristine trekking paths, and experience unmatched hospitality.

Whether you are planning a weekend camping expedition with friends or a tranquil retreat into nature, our grounds provide the ultimate scenic basecamp.

What We Offer

⛺ Tent Camping

Comfortable, secure camping setups right in the heart of nature under the open skies of Basar.

🔥 Campfire Nights

Unwind with warmth, music, and stories around a cozy evening bonfire with fellow travelers.

🌲 Nature Trails

Guided or self-explored trekking pathways offering rich scenic views of Leparada's greenery.
